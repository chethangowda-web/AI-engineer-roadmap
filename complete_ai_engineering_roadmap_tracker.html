Video Summary: Complete AI Engineering Roadmap

By Krish Naik • Reference Guide & Career Breakdown

In this comprehensive roadmap video, Krish Naik breaks down the exact skills required to transition from a traditional developer or data scientist into a production-ready AI Engineer, LLMOps Engineer, or AI Forward Deployed Engineer.

1. Software & AI Foundations (Stages 1–2)

AI Engineering is 70% software engineering. Mastering Async Python, FastAPI, Pydantic, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, and core ML/DL/Transformer mechanics is mandatory before building complex LLM pipelines.

2. Context, Apps & Advanced RAG (Stages 3–5)

Shifts from simple prompt engineering to Context Engineering, structured outputs, tool calling, semantic caching, and production RAG (Hybrid Search, Reranking, GraphRAG, CRAG, Self-RAG, Agentic RAG).

3. AI Agents, MCP & Orchestration (Stages 6–7)

Covers autonomous reasoning (ReAct, Reflection), memory architectures, the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and multi-agent state orchestration with LangGraph, Google ADK, OpenAI Agents SDK, CrewAI, and PydanticAI.

4. Evals, Security, Ops & Design (Stages 8–13)

Enterprise readiness requires automated Evals, OpenTelemetry/LangSmith tracing, Guardrails against prompt injection, GenAI Ops (prompt/model versioning), Cloud services (Bedrock, Vertex AI, Azure), and System Design trade-offs.
